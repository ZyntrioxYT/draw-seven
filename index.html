THE SHORT VERSION

How to play

01

Draw or stay. On your turn, take one card or bank your round value.

02

Avoid repeats. Draw a number already in your row and you bust, scoring zero that round.

03

Find seven. Collect seven different number cards to end the round and earn a 15-point bonus.

04

Race to 200. Once anyone reaches 200 at the end of a round, the highest total wins.

Special cards

Second Chance absorbs one duplicate. Freeze forces an active player to stay. Flip Three makes an active player take three cards. Score cards add +2, +4, +6, +8, +10, or double your number total.
